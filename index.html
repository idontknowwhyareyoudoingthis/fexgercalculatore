How the math works

Gear ratio — how many times the rear wheel turns for each pedal revolution.
Skid patches — the number of different spots on your rear tire that can contact the ground during a skid.
Estimated crank RPM — the calculator works backwards from your entered speed to estimate how fast your pedals turn.
Wheel RPM — calculated from your speed and selected tire size.

Tire measurements are estimated automatically from your wheel size and tire width, so you don't need to measure or enter them.
